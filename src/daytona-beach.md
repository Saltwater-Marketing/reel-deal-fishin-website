**Daytona Beach visitors fish the same water we do.** We launch in New Smyrna Beach, a short drive south, with the fastest access to the Indian River Lagoon, the bridges, the inlet and the nearshore bite.

Our August 2026 trips covered the waters around Daytona Beach and New Smyrna Beach and produced excellent snook, slot to oversized redfish and mixed inshore bags ([August report](/fishing-reports/new-smyrna-beach-fishing-report-august-2026)). In March, the bridges from New Smyrna Beach up to Port Orange held sheepshead all month ([March report](/fishing-reports/new-smyrna-beach-fishing-report-march-2026)).

## Inshore Fishing Near Daytona Beach

[Inshore trips](/inshore) stay inside protected water where the fishing is steady. We sight cast the flats, drift edges and work mangroves, docks and bridges for redfish, snook, trout, sheepshead and flounder. It's calm, close and great for kids and first timers.

## Nearshore and Shark Fishing Near Daytona

When conditions allow, we run just outside the inlet and fish a few miles off the beach for kingfish, cobia, tarpon, jacks and sharks. The rides are short and you stay in sight of the shoreline. See [nearshore fishing](/nearshore) and [shark fishing](/shark-fishing).

## After Dark

Capt. Chad is one of the only guides in the Daytona area offering [flounder gigging](/flounder-gigging) from the boat, and our [bioluminescence tours](/bio-tour) light up the lagoon on warm summer nights.

## Best Time of Year

- **Spring:** redfish, trout, black drum, cobia, kingfish
- **Summer:** snook, tarpon, sharks, trout
- **Fall:** redfish, flounder, drum, kingfish
- **Winter:** sheepshead, drum, cold water redfish
