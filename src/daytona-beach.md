**Staying in Daytona Beach? You're fishing the same water we do.** [Capt. Chad Knight](/about) fishes the Halifax River, the Port Orange bridges, Ponce Inlet and the beaches between Daytona and New Smyrna Beach, chasing sharks, bull reds, snook and flounder.

We launch in New Smyrna Beach, a short drive south, so a Daytona stay and a private fishing charter fit in the same morning.

## What's Biting Near Daytona

Big reds run all through the fall around New Smyrna Beach and Daytona Beach, from slot fish to oversized bulls ([October report](/fishing-reports/new-smyrna-beach-fishing-report-october-2026)). Late summer fishing trips around Daytona have produced snook and slot to oversized reds ([August report](/fishing-reports/new-smyrna-beach-fishing-report-august-2026)).

In winter the bridges and docks from New Smyrna Beach up to Port Orange fill with sheepshead. March trips have put multiple limits over the rail ([March report](/fishing-reports/new-smyrna-beach-fishing-report-march-2026)).

## Pick Your Trip

- **[Inshore fishing](/inshore):** calm, protected water for redfish, snook, trout, sheepshead and flounder. Great for kids and first timers.
- **[Redfish charters](/redfish):** slot fish and oversized bulls, every month of the year.
- **[Shark fishing](/shark-fishing):** blacktips, spinners and more just off the beach, May 1 to November 1.
- **[Nearshore fishing](/nearshore):** up to 6 miles out for kingfish, flounder and sand trout.
- **[Dolphin & manatee tours](/dolphin):** a slow 2 hour cruise for guests who'd rather watch than fish.

## Best Time of Year

- **Spring:** redfish, trout, black drum, cobia and kingfish
- **Summer:** snook, tarpon, sharks and trout
- **Fall:** oversized redfish, flounder, drum and kingfish
- **Winter:** sheepshead and drum on the bridges, plus cold water redfish
