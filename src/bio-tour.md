**Welcome to nature's most magical light show.** When the sun goes down and the water starts glowing, you'll understand why bioluminescence is one of Florida's best kept secrets, and why this tour is a must for locals and visitors alike.

## After Dark, the Magic Begins

We launch at twilight, cruising quiet backwaters under a sky full of stars. As we drift through the Indian River Lagoon, every splash, paddle and wake lights up with electric blue sparkles.

The glow comes from microscopic organisms called dinoflagellates, tiny plankton that shimmer when stirred. It's like watching underwater fireworks, and you've got the best seat in the house.

*Best viewing is on warm, moonless nights. Ask us for peak times!*
