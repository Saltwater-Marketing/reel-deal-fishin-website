**Forget the theme parks. This is the real Florida.** The Indian River Lagoon is one of the most biodiverse estuaries in North America, and the slow way through it is the best way to meet its most famous residents.

## Dolphin & Manatee Tour

A 2 hour cruise starting at 9:00 AM. We go slow and stay shallow for a front row seat to leaping dolphins, gliding manatees and the occasional bald eagle overhead, without disturbing their natural rhythm. Want longer? Extra time can be added up to 24 hours before your trip.

<h2 id="bioluminescence">Bioluminescence Tours (Seasonal)</h2>

When the water is warm and the glow is on, Capt. Chad runs night tours through the quiet backwaters. We launch at twilight, and every splash and wake lights up electric blue. The glow comes from microscopic plankton called dinoflagellates that shimmer when the water is stirred.

These tours only run at certain times of year, mostly summer into early fall, on nights when conditions are right. **Call or text Capt. Chad at [(386) 853-4814](tel:+13868534814) to ask about upcoming glow nights.**

Want to catch a few too? An [inshore fishing trip](/inshore) runs in the same calm lagoon water.
