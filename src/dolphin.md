**Forget the theme parks. This is the real Florida.** Our Dolphin & Manatee Tour is a calm water cruise through the Indian River Lagoon, one of the most biodiverse estuaries in North America and home to two of its most beloved residents.

## Where Wildlife Steals the Show

This isn't just a boat ride. It's a front row seat to leaping dolphins, slow gliding manatees and the occasional bald eagle overhead.

We cruise slowly and stay shallow, giving you a chance to see these gentle giants up close without disturbing their natural rhythm. Bring your camera, bring your kids and bring your curiosity. This trip is as peaceful as it is wild.

## Book Your Wildlife Tour

Want to see the lagoon after dark too? Pair it with a [bioluminescence tour](/bio-tour) on a warm summer night.
