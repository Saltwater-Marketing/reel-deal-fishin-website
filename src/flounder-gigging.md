**No rods. No reels. No sunscreen.** Just underwater lights, calm water and your chance to strike.

On December 15, 2025, the crew from Boat Outfitters went out on a calm, clear night and filled the deck with limits of quality flounder ([December report](/fishing-reports/new-smyrna-beach-fishing-report-december-2025)). Earlier that month, another crew stuck their first fish on the very first drift. That's what a good gigging night looks like.

Capt. Chad is one of the only guides in the Daytona and New Smyrna area who offers flounder gigging from the boat, and it's been his specialty for over a decade.

## What a Gigging Night Is Like

We light up the shallows of the Indian River Lagoon like a stage. Custom halogen and LED rigs cut through the dark and reveal the bottom, and you stand on the bow scanning for the outline of a flounder.

When you see one, you strike. No casting and no bait, just a multi prong gig and a steady hand. Most of it happens just feet from shore, in inches of water, with no waves and no long boat rides.

## Family Friendly. Crowd Free.

- Private charter, your group only
- Calm inland water with no seasickness
- Child size life vests and a quick Flounder 101 before we start
- No UV exposure, so no sunscreen required

## Gigging From Jacksonville to New Smyrna Beach

We run gigging trips along Florida's east coast from southern Jacksonville to St. Augustine, Palm Coast and New Smyrna Beach, depending on the time of year and conditions. Clear water and calm wind make the best nights, and September 2026 produced solid catches headed for the dinner table ([September report](/fishing-reports/new-smyrna-beach-fishing-report-september-2026)).

Prefer to fish for flounder by day? They're part of our [inshore trips](/inshore). Want another night on the water? Try a [bioluminescence tour](/bio-tour) on a warm summer night.
