We spent a February morning fishing off the end of the jetty in New Smyrna Beach, and the kids barely had time to sit down. Schools of pompano kept sliding through on the current, and the action stayed steady once we found them.

### **Pompano**

Clean water and steady current set it up. Seas stayed manageable, and the fish were stacked tight and feeding hard. That led to quick limits and plenty of bent rods without long waits between hookups.

Seeing the kids get in on it was the highlight. Pompano runs don't last all winter, so when they line up like this, it's worth going.

### **Sheepshead**

Cold water flipped the switch on our other favorite winter bite. Sheepshead were thick around docks, bridges, pilings and rock piles, right where the crustaceans move in when the water cools.

Slow presentations and natural baits were the key. Drop it straight down, stay patient and be ready, because most bites feel like a light tap before the rod loads up.

**Captain's tip:** watch your line, not just the rod tip. Sheepshead are famous bait thieves, and the ones that get hooked usually eat fast and subtle.

### **Who These Trips Suit**

Families, beginners and anyone who wants steady action without a long run. Sheepshead fight harder than people expect, and the firm white fillets are great on the grill or in the fryer. More on [winter sheepshead trips](/inshore#sheepshead).
