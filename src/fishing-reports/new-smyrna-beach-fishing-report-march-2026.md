March was a sheepshead month from start to finish. Trips around the bridges and docks of New Smyrna Beach and nearby Port Orange put multiple limits over the rail, and black drum took over the late month action.

### **Sheepshead**

The fish stacked up on bridge pilings, docks and other structure in the Indian River Lagoon. Live shrimp and fiddler crabs worked tight to the pilings did most of the work, and the bite held up through the tide changes.

Patience paid off. Sheepshead have a light, subtle bite, and learning to feel that tap and set the hook at the right moment is half the fun. Once a crew figured it out, the action came quickly, with plenty of solid keepers.

### **Black Drum**

By the end of the month, sheepshead and black drum were sharing the docks, pilings and heavier structure. Drop a bait in the right spot and it didn't take long to feel that thump and watch the rod load up.

### **Sharks and Jacks**

Early in the month, a family trip tangled with a couple of sharks between sheepshead, which kept everyone on their toes. Later on, jack crevalle crashed through the structure bite and a few more sharks showed up to keep things interesting.

### **The Crews**

The kids ran the show this month. On one trip, the young anglers took over the rods and reeled in fish like seasoned pros. Families, groups of friends and first timers all got in on the action and went home with fish for the table.

### **Looking Ahead to April**

Spring was lining up the way it should. As the water warms, expect the snook and redfish bite to build around the bridges and inlet. Read about [sheepshead fishing on the bridges](/inshore#sheepshead).
