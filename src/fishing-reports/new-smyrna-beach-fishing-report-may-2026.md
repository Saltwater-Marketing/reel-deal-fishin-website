Dock lights on the water, bait moving across the surface, then the thump. That was the snook bite after dark in early May, and those night trips gave us some of the most consistent action we'd seen in a while.

By mid month the bridges had taken over, and multiple trophy class snook hit the deck in a single week.

### **Snook**

The first week, we fished bridges, docks and deeper edges around the Indian River Lagoon. Snook were stacked tight to structure and ate live bait early in the morning and right before dark. Plenty of slot fish, with bigger ones mixed in, and once one committed there was no second chance.

The pattern was simple: find structure, fish it slow and be ready, because when they ate it was quick.

Around May 19, the bridges with steady current were the place to be. Early mornings with moving water produced, and live bait around the structure and current lines got the best results. When the tide started moving, these fish struck hard and made long runs. That stretch ran from the Indian River Lagoon to spots near Daytona Beach.

Late in May, docks and deeper channel edges gave up several upper slot and oversized snook, including a few absolute tanks.

The kids held their own. One young angler handled a solid snook like a pro.

### **Redfish**

May opened with a family down from Canada, fishing these waters for the first time. Conditions started a little mixed, with light wind and moving water, but once we set up on the right structure it changed quick.

They got into multiple quality reds, and a couple of those fish made them earn it with strong runs. There were a few "no way!" moments on the boat that day.

Through the rest of the month, quality redfish showed up trip after trip. Clean water around the inlet kept the bite consistent, especially early before the afternoon storms built.

### **Weather and Timing**

Late May gave us a little bit of everything weatherwise, but the fish stayed fired up. Families and first timers were in on it, and more than one first fish turned into a personal best with the parents taking pictures.

The play was the morning, before the sun got high and before the storms built. That early bite was worth the alarm clock. More on [snook fishing in New Smyrna Beach](/inshore#snook).
