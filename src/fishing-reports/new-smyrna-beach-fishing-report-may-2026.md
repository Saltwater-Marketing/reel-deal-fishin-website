The biggest change in May: snook moved from staging on the edges to stacking up on the bridges, and the bite went from good to trophy class. Multiple trophy snook hit the deck during the month.

### **What Changed: The Bridges Turned On**

Early mornings with moving water were the window. We focused on bridges with steady current, and live bait around the structure and current lines got the best results. When the tide moved, these fish struck hard and made long, drag pulling runs.

Plenty of slot fish were around, with bigger ones mixed in. By late May, several upper slot and oversized snook came off the docks and deeper channel edges.

### **What Changed: The Night Bite**

Once the light dropped and the water settled, the snook turned on. Dock lights on the surface, bait moving, then that thump. Night trips produced some of the most consistent action we'd seen in a while.

### **Redfish Held Steady**

The month opened with a family from Canada on their first Florida trip. Conditions started a little mixed, but once we set up on the right structure, they got into multiple quality redfish that earned every bit of the fight.

Through the rest of May, quality reds showed up trip after trip. Clean water around the inlet kept the action consistent, especially early before the afternoon storms built.

### **What It Means for Your Trip**

- Book early: the morning bite before the sun climbs has been the strongest
- Consider an evening trip for snook under the lights
- Kids are doing great: one young angler handled a solid snook like a pro, and others landed personal bests

Summer patterns were settling in by the end of the month. More on [snook fishing in New Smyrna Beach](/inshore#snook).
