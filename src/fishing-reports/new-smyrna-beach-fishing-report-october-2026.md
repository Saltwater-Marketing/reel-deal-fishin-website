A young angler battling their first big redfish set the tone for October. The rod doubled over, the drag started screaming, and nobody knew how big that fish was until it came alongside the boat.

### **Oversized Redfish**

Big reds were the story all month. We landed beautiful oversized fish around New Smyrna Beach and Daytona Beach, giving guests a real feel for how much power these fish have. Family trips had kids and adults alike getting in on the action.

### **Black Drum and Snook**

Black drum made regular appearances in our local waters, adding variety to the inshore trips, and we had chances at snook too. Between New Smyrna Beach, Mosquito Lagoon and the Indian River Lagoon, every trip brought something a little different.

### **After Dark**

Night trips offered a completely different way to fish, with snook and other local species after dark. And our signature [flounder gigging](/flounder-gigging) trips cruised the shallows under bright lights, spotting flounder along the bottom.

### **The Crews**

Some guests had fished their whole lives. Others picked up a rod for the very first time. Either way, Capt. Chad got everyone involved, and the smiles in this month's photos say it all. Book an [inshore trip](/inshore) for the fall redfish run.
