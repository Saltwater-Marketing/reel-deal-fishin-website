The first drop of an early December morning went to a young angler in a blue and yellow life jacket, and he went right to work. By the time he sat down for a photo, a whole row of lane snapper was lined up on the deck beside him.

A light breeze, calm seas and a perfect drift made it an easy morning to stay on them. Here's how December went, species by species.

### **Lane Snapper and Red Snapper**

The lane snapper were stacked up early. They chewed on just about every spot we hit, and the bite held steady right through the morning with quick bites and very little downtime.

We finished that trip with a beautiful red snapper.

### **Sharks**

Sharks moved in on that same morning, and the energy on the boat jumped. Fast runs, tight drag and a lot of wide eyes.

The crew got to hold their very first shark for a quick photo.

### **Sheepshead**

By the middle of the month the sheepshead bite was firing in New Smyrna Beach. Most of the fish were holding on docks, bridges and nearshore structure.

As the water cooled, they stacked up tight to that structure and stayed aggressive all day. Live shrimp and fiddler crabs were the top producers.

The anglers who slowed down and took their time working those baits around the pilings got consistent bites and solid fish.

### **Flounder**

Our flounder this month came on night gigging trips. Early in December the water settled out smooth and crystal clear, and the flats lit up under the lights.

That crew stuck a fish on the first drift and kept going, one after another, and finished the night with a cooler of thick, healthy flatfish.

In mid December the boys from Boat Outfitters went out on a night with calm wind, clear water and excellent visibility. Sharp eyes and good teamwork filled the deck with limits of quality flounder.

The photos below show both sides of the month: one angler crouched on the dock at night holding a flounder with more laid out at her feet, and a crew of three on the dock by daylight holding flounder over a spread of sheepshead and flatfish.

### **What Lined Up This Month**

Every trip in this report had the same thing going for it: calm wind and clean, clear water. Those were the days that produced, by daylight on the structure and after dark on the flats.

Sheepshead on the pilings were the steadiest bite heading out of December. See how we fish them on our [inshore trips](/inshore#sheepshead).
