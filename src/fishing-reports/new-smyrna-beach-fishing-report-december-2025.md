A young angler started putting lane snapper in the cooler on the very first drop this month, and that set the tone for December. Light breeze, calm seas and a good drift made for an easy start.

### **Snapper and Sharks**

The snapper kept chewing on just about every spot we hit that morning. Then the sharks showed up, and the energy jumped: fast runs, tight drag and a crew holding their very first shark for a quick photo.

We wrapped that trip with a beautiful red snapper.

### **Sheepshead**

By mid month the sheepshead bite was firing. Fish were holding on docks, bridges and nearshore structure, stacking up tight as the water cooled and staying aggressive through the day.

Live shrimp and fiddler crabs were the top producers. Anglers who slowed down and worked those baits around the pilings got steady bites and solid fish.

### **Flounder Gigging**

Two gigging nights stood out. Early in the month the water settled out smooth and crystal clear, and the crew stuck a fish on the first drift. By the end of the night the cooler held a lineup of thick, healthy flatfish.

On December 15, the crew from Boat Outfitters went out on a calm, clear night with excellent visibility. Sharp eyes and good teamwork filled the deck with limits of quality flounder.

### **What It Means for Your Trip**

Cooler water pushes sheepshead tight to structure, which makes for simple, steady fishing that kids pick up fast.
